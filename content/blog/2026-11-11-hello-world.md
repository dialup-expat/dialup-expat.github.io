I decided to start a blog/journal in the year 2026. I know, how quaint. But that's precisely the point.

It turns out that my aging brain longs for the old internet. For the excitement of the 90s when the information superhighway was brand new. Where the world wide web was an unregulated frontier where everyone could participate and stake their claim. Long before the over commercialization of everything pixel and interaction. Where people had particular skillsets or passions and they aimed it toward building destinations that had something to offer.

Today that internet still exists, stuck between the pages of a never-ending deluge of clickbait and advertisements. There are websites still out there where people who still share their passion, if you just try to find it.

What is this blog going to be about? Nothing. Everything. But not [everything/nothing](@/blog/2026-11-12-everything-nothing.md); or maybe so? Essentially, whatever I want to write about, whenever I want to write it, until I eventually get bored and stop writing, much like I do with every other project scattered about my digital and physical life.

Who's going to read it? Likely no one. Maybe some agents out there scouring the web for ever more training data. I guess this one's for the robots.

So welcome here, and [hello, world](@/blog/2026-11-13-of-programming-and-progz.md).
